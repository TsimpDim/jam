# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Active job seekers managing 10–50+ applications simultaneously. They're often stressed, tired from the search grind, and juggling multiple tools (spreadsheets, job boards, notes). Their context is fragmented attention — checking statuses between interviews, on mobile during commutes, at a desk when applying. They need a tool that reduces cognitive load and makes them feel in control, not one that adds more noise.

## Product Purpose

JAM! organizes the chaos of job hunting into one calm, capable command center. Track applications, generate cover letters with AI, discover matching leads, and see your entire journey at a glance. Success is when a user opens JAM! and immediately knows where every application stands — no spreadsheets, no scattered notes, no anxiety — just clarity and forward momentum.

## Brand Personality

**Energetic, motivating, crafted.** The exclamation mark isn't decoration — it's the energy. JAM! is your sharp, enthusiastic ally in the job hunt: opinionated enough to guide you, personal enough to feel like yours. Think Arc Browser's crafted intimacy applied to a productivity tool — not cold SaaS, not terminal-aesthetic, not gamified candy.

## Anti-references

- **Corporate HR SaaS**: Cold blues, stock photos of handshakes, sterile dashboards that feel like enterprise compliance tools
- **Dev-tool dark mode**: Terminal aesthetics, monospace everywhere, feels like a CLI not a companion
- **Playful/gamified**: Candy colors, XP bars, achievement popups — a tool, not a toy

## Design Principles

1. **Energy without chaos** — Motivating and lively, but organized and calm. The tool reduces anxiety, never adds to it.
2. **Crafted, not corporate** — Arc-like opinionated polish. Every interaction feels intentional. No generic SaaS defaults.
3. **All your applications, one glance** — The core value proposition: see everything at once, reduce cognitive load. Status, stage, and next steps surfaced instantly.
4. **Confidence through clarity** — Never wonder "where am I in this process?" Progress is visible, next steps are obvious, control is unambiguous.
5. **Move the seeker forward** — Every feature earns its place by helping the user take the next step toward landing a job. No feature-creep, no dashboard-as-endpoint.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target: WCAG 2.1 AA across all surfaces
- Reduced motion support for all animations
- Keyboard-navigable for primary workflows
- Color is never the sole indicator of status or state
- Mobile-responsive — the tool must work on the go, during commutes, between interviews
