About JAM!

JAM! was born from a simple frustration: keeping track of multiple job applications shouldn't be this hard. We've all been there - applying to dozens of positions, losing track of where we applied, missing follow-ups, and feeling overwhelmed by the entire process. Is my CV correct? Did I enter too much information? What exactly where the role details?

We believe that job searching is challenging enough without having to juggle spreadsheets, sticky notes, and scattered email threads. That's why we created JAM! - a powerful yet simple tool that puts you back in control of your job search journey.

Our Mission

To empower every job seeker with the tools they need to organize, track, and succeed in their job search. We're committed to making the job application process less stressful and more strategic, helping you focus on what really matters: landing your dream job.

Our Vision

By 2030, JAM! will be a cornerstone of every job hunt. Key features will allow users to completely centralize their job search and required integrations with major platforms will be in place for seamless exchange of data - allowing for interoperability of tools when needed.

Our Values

Simplicity: Complex tools create friction. We design for clarity and ease of use, so you can focus on your job search, not on learning software.

Privacy: Your job search is personal. Your data belongs to you, period.

Accessibility: Everyone deserves access to great tools. That's why JAM! is free forever for basic use, with affordable premium options for those who need more.

Community: We're built by job seekers, for job seekers. Your feedback shapes our roadmap, and your success drives our motivation.
